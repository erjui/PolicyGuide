The research

Abstract

Policy compliance depends on completing required steps as well as avoiding prohibited actions. Checking only the final action can discover a missing prerequisite after an agent has already skipped the procedure.

PolicyGuide compiles a domain policy into reusable workflow graphs. At user-turn boundaries, a verifier reconciles open requests, checks grounded evidence, and advances each request to its first unmet requirement. It returns guidance and maintains a gate on policy-sensitive actions. Experiments evaluate task reliability in the airline, retail, and telecom domains, together with cross-model transfer and procedural compliance.

Method

Follow the policy across the whole request.

Offline workflow construction and persistent online tracking give the verifier a procedural view of the task.
